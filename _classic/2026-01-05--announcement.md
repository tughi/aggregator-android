After a long hiatus, development on the classic Aggregator app has been resumed.

The classic app has always been a simple, no-nonsense feed reader, and that's exactly what it will remain. Updates will focus on keeping it compatible with modern Android versions and fixing issues reported by users.

If you have feedback, head over to the [discussions](https://github.com/tughi/aggregator-android/discussions) section on GitHub.
